The Problem

Seeing isn't the problem.
Knowing what to do is.

Most AI vision assistants describe what's in front of the camera. But visually impaired users don't need a list of objects — they need actionable spatial guidance.

Most AI assistants answer
"I see a chair, table, bottle, person, plant and a door."
Object listsNot helpfulContext missing
What users actually need
"The chair is 2.1 metres ahead. Slightly left. Move forward."
ActionableSpatial contextRare in AI
The Insight
The real value isn't a description of the scene — it's an understanding of your intent and actionable guidance to achieve it.
Same Scene

Object-centric

"What do you see?" — the camera sees the same scene. Traditional AI generates a list of objects.

Different Question

Intent-centric — SIGHTLINE

"What am I trying to do?" — detect, reason in space, then guide: "The chair is 2.1 metres ahead, slightly left."
